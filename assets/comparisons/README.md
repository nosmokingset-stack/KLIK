# Approved KLIK comparison

The supplied, user-approved digital illustration was split by cropping only. No button shape, hand, machine or water stream was generated or retouched. Original embedded titles, central arrow and outer frames are excluded from the exported crops. Titles, captions, frames and transition arrow are implemented in HTML/CSS/SVG and localized in site.js.

The comparison is a demonstration visualization, not documentary evidence of product testing or compatibility. This qualification is visible below the comparison in both languages. Original high-resolution PNG and crop masters remain local.

`before-720.webp` and `after-720.webp` are 714×640 pixels; `*-480.webp` are responsive smaller versions. The 720 filename denotes the size class, not an upscaled image.
